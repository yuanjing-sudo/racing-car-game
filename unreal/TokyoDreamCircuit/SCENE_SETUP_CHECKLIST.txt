1. Create Content/Maps/TokyoNightRun.
2. Add TDCProceduralRoad at origin and press Build Default Circuit.
3. Add TDCPlayerCar above the road.
4. Press Play. The game mode spawns ATDCGoldenHour (sun, sky, fog, grade),
   ATDCWorldLandmarks (Tokyo Tower, Skytree, Fuji, Rainbow Bridge, torii,
   220-tower skyline) and ATDCWeatherSystem (rain, wheel spray, wet grip).
   To place them by hand instead, drop the three actors in and uncheck
   bSpawnNeonHorizonWorld on the game mode.
5. Optional, biggest win: assign a wet asphalt material to the road and expose
   the scalars Wetness, RainIntensity and PuddleMask. The weather system writes
   them every second. Without it the scene still works.
6. Optional: import Nanite city/tower/Fuji meshes and a real hypercar to replace
   the procedural stand-ins. See NEON_HORIZON.md for what is deliberately absent.
7. Optional: replace the placeholder rigid body with Chaos Vehicles for
   suspension and tyre physics.
